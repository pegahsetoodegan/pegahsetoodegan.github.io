About me

I’m an interaction designer with a background in industrial design, visual communication, and art direction.

I was born and raised in Iran, then moved to France, and later lived and worked in China before coming to Budapest. Moving between different countries, languages, and cultures has strongly shaped the way I observe people, environments, and systems.

My path into interaction design grew naturally from this curiosity. I first studied industrial design, then visual arts in Paris, and spent several years working across art direction and visual communication in private companies, cultural institutions, and public services.

Today, through my MA in Interaction Design at MOME, I am expanding this practice toward UX, design research, service design, and complex digital experiences.

What connects these different parts of my practice is an interest in concepts, research, systems thinking, and visual direction. I like to understand the context behind a problem before designing its form.

Travel and cultural observation remain an important part of how I think. I’m curious about how people live, communicate, adapt, and create different ways of doing things.
